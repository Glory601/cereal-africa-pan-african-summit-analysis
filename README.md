# Cereal+ Africa Pan African Summit — Registration Analytics

> **Data Analytics Portfolio Project | Campaign Performance & Registration Insights**

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes registration data for the **Cereal+ Africa Pan African Summit** to evaluate campaign performance, understand participant demographics, identify effective registration channels, and track progress toward the registration target.

The project transforms registration data into **clear business insights through data analysis and Power BI visualization**, helping stakeholders understand what is working and where further campaign action is needed.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The summit team needed a clear and reliable way to monitor registration performance throughout the campaign.

The analysis focused on answering key business questions:

- How many participants registered?
- How close were registrations to the target?
- Which channels generated the most registrations?
- Who were the major participant groups?
- Where were participants registering from?
- How did registration performance change over time?
- What actions could improve registration performance?
---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

2. Analyze participant categories and geographic distribution.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Registration Performance

The campaign recorded **68 total registrations**, achieving **85% of the 80-registration target**.

### Week 4 Growth

Week 4 recorded **22 registrations**, representing **69% growth** and making it an important period of campaign momentum.

### Registration Sources

**WhatsApp** was the strongest registration source, generating **37 registrations**, followed by **word of mouth with 10 registrations**.

### Participant Categories

**Agribusiness owners** represented the largest participant category with **17 registrations**, followed by commercial farmers with **13** and smallholder farmers with **11**.

### Geographic Reach

Nigeria accounted for the majority of registrations, while the campaign also attracted participants from other countries across Africa.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

1. **Prioritize WhatsApp outreach** as a major registration acquisition channel.
2. **Continue targeting agribusiness owners and commercial farmers**, who represented strong participant segments.
3. **Analyze the drivers of Week 4 growth** and replicate successful campaign activities.
4. **Strengthen outreach in lower-performing geographic markets** to improve regional participation.
5. **Continue monitoring registration performance regularly** to identify gaps early and support timely campaign decisions.
